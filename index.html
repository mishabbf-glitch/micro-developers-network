About Us

Experienced security consulting, built on trust

Micro Developers Network is a Ghana-based security consultancy providing corporate security, smart home automation, executive protection and event security. We work with businesses, homeowners and event organizers to design protection that fits the actual risk — not a one-size-fits-all package.

Every engagement starts with an assessment, is delivered by vetted personnel, and is backed by a direct line to our team on call and WhatsApp. Our security guarding operations are structured in line with ISO 18788, the international management-system standard for private security operations.
